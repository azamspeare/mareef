# pricing.md

## 1. Page Purpose

This page presents Mareef’s subscription model. It helps users compare Free, Premium, and VIP tiers and understand what’s included in each.

The pricing page should clearly communicate:
- What each plan offers
- Who each plan is best for
- How Mareef scales with teacher needs
- That all task types are included in all plans
- That AI features and automation are only in paid plans
- That the product is currently free during early access

### Swiper content
#### 1. ⏳ "Saved 6+ hours/week on grading"
Private tutors using Mareef’s writing workflow reduced manual feedback time by 50–70%.

#### 2. 🧠 "Better student engagement"
Teachers report students submit on time more consistently with Mareef’s structured writing flow.

#### 3. 🔁 "90% task feedback visibility"
Every student sees their teacher’s comments — no more ignored Google Docs or lost chats.

#### 4. 🧪 "Over 700 tasks created in private beta"
Teachers from 5+ countries tested Mareef to streamline writing instruction.

#### 5. 🌍 "Supports 10+ languages"
English, Arabic, Korean, Russian, and more — Mareef works for every language teacher.

## 2. Plans

### Free Plan
- ✅ 1 teacher account
- ✅ 0 assistants
- ✅ Up to 20 students
- ✅ All task types included (writing, reading, speaking, listening)
- ✅ Manual workflow (create, assign, review)
- ✅ Basic analytics
- ✅ Email notifications

> Best for solo teachers just getting started

---

### Premium Plan
- ✅ 1 teacher account
- ✅ Up to 3 assistants
- ✅ Up to 200 students
- ✅ All task types
- ✅ AI task generator (planned)
- ✅ AI feedback assistant (planned)
- ✅ Advanced analytics (planned)
- ✅ Email notifications

> Ideal for tutors or teachers managing larger student groups with assistants

**Planned price**: ~$29/month

---

### VIP Plan
- ✅ Up to 10 teacher accounts
- ✅ Up to 20 assistants
- ✅ Up to 500 students
- ✅ Everything in Premium
- ✅ Designed for schools, academies, and small teaching teams

**Planned price**: ~$49/month

---

## 3. Notes

- ✅ All task types (writing, reading, listening, speaking) are included in every plan
- 🛠 AI features are under development and marked as “Coming Soon”
- 💸 Pricing is not enforced yet — all plans are free during early access
- 📅 Billing will be monthly, no long-term commitment
- 🧪 Early adopters may receive extended free access

## 4. Design Instructions

- Use a 3-column pricing card layout (Free | Premium | VIP)
- Highlight the Free plan with a “Start free” badge
- Mark Premium and VIP with “Coming Soon” on features that are still in dev
- Add a callout banner at the bottom:
  > “All features are currently free while we’re in early access. You’ll be notified before any billing begins.”
- Include a CTA under each card:
  - Free: “Start for Free”
  - Premium/VIP: “Join Waitlist”
