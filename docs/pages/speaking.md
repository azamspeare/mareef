## 2. The Problem

Speaking assignments are one of the hardest to manage:

- Students send voice messages through Telegram or WhatsApp
- Teachers reply with general comments — no timestamps, no structure
- No version history or way to track pronunciation progress
- Feedback often gets ignored or forgotten

It's messy, unscalable, and hard to use for real teaching growth.

---

## 3. Our Solution

Mareff’s Speaking Tasks will offer a structured voice submission workflow:

- Students record audio directly in the browser
- Optional word limit or topic instructions
- Teachers listen and leave timestamped feedback
- Score and feedback are saved with the recording
- All history is stored — every attempt, every review

It’s like a speaking test, without the scheduling headaches.
