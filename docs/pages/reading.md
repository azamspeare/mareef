## 2. The Problem

Reading comprehension is one of the most common language tasks — but it’s still handled with paper printouts or makeshift Google Forms.

- Teachers manually prepare PDFs or scans
- Students return messy answers
- Teachers spend hours grading each one
- No data, no tracking, no history

In 2025, this still eats up hours a week.

---

## 3. Our Solution

Mareef’s upcoming Reading Tasks will change that:

- Upload or write your reading material directly in the editor
- Add comprehension questions (multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, short answer)
- Set timers or reading durations
- Students answer inside the task — no separate docs or platforms
- Auto-grading for close-ended questions
- Feedback and analytics built-in

Designed to feel like a real classroom worksheet — but smarter.
