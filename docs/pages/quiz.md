## The Problem

Traditional quizzes are harder than they should be.

Teachers usually:
- Create quizzes in Google Forms, Word, or on paper
- Manually manage links, answers, and submissions
- Re-check results across different tools
- Lose historical data and student progress over time

Even for simple knowledge checks, teachers spend too much time creating, checking, and managing quizzes instead of teaching.

---

## Our Solution

Mareef Quizzes are built as a **native task type**, not a separate tool.

With Quiz Tasks, teachers can:
- Create quizzes directly inside Mareef
- Assign them to students with a single link
- Automatically evaluate answers
- Instantly see results, scores, and progress

No extra tools.  
No copy-paste workflows.  
No manual checking.

---

## Quiz Types (MVP)

Mareef supports practical quiz formats designed for fast adoption:

- **Single Choice** — one correct answer
- **Multiple Choice** — multiple correct answers
- **True / False** — quick concept checks
- **Short Answer** — brief text responses
- **Long Answer** — explanations or reasoning (manual review)

More formats will be added over time without breaking existing quizzes.

## How It Works

1. **Create a Quiz**  
   Choose quiz type, add questions, and set correct answers.

2. **Assign to Students**  
   Share a secure link or assign directly to a group.

3. **Students Complete the Quiz**  
   Clean, distraction-free interface.

4. **Automatic Evaluation**  
   Objective questions are graded instantly.

5. **Review Results & Analytics**  
   See scores, completion rates, and student progress.
