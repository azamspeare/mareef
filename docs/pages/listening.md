## 2. The Problem

Listening tasks are messy and disconnected:

- Teachers send audio files in Telegram or upload to YouTube
- Questions are sent separately in Google Docs or paper
- Students answer in random formats — chat, email, handwritten
- There’s no way to align listening time with student responses
- Feedback is slow or nonexistent

It’s disconnected, and teachers waste time re-linking everything.

---

## 3. Our Solution

Mareef’s Listening Tasks will be built specifically for language teachers:

- Upload audio files or embed links
- Write timed or general comprehension questions
- Optional playback restrictions (1x listen, no rewind)
- Students answer directly beneath the player
- Auto-evaluate where possible
- Full review + feedback panel for teacher

Everything in one task — no extra apps, no tracking confusion.
