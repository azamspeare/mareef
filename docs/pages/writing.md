## 2. The Problem

Managing writing assignments is a mess:

- Teachers assign tasks in Google Docs or chats
- Students reply with documents or messages
- Feedback is scattered across emails and comments
- No history, no structure, no analytics

It’s slow. It’s manual. And no one enjoys it.

---

## 3. Our Solution

Mareef gives writing tasks a dedicated workflow.

- Teachers create writing tasks with structured instructions
- Students write directly inside the Mareef editor — no file uploads
- Teachers give inline feedback and assign scores
- Students get notified when feedback is ready

Every submission is saved. Every comment is tracked.
Writing becomes repeatable, not chaotic.
